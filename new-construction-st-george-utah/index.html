How I Help New-Construction Buyers

1. Before you visit a model home

Many builders want your agent with you or registered at your first visit, though policies vary. Call me before you tour so you're represented from day one.

2. Comparing builders and communities

We'll compare floor plans, lot premiums, HOA rules and fees, included features versus upgrades, and resale homes nearby, so you know what you're really getting for the price.

3. Reviewing the builder contract

Builder contracts are written by the builder. I'll help you understand deposits, deadlines, change orders, and what happens if construction is delayed. For legal questions, talk with a real estate attorney.

4. Inspections, even on a new home

I recommend an independent inspection. A phase inspection before drywall goes up and a final inspection before closing let you catch issues while the builder can still fix them.

5. Through closing and the walkthrough

I'll help you with the final walkthrough, the punch list, and the warranty paperwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own real estate agent to buy a new-construction home?

It's a good idea. A builder's on-site sales staff represents the builder, while your own buyer's agent represents you in pricing, contract terms, and inspections. Compensation is set in your written buyer agreement.

Should I get a home inspection on a brand-new home in St. George?

Yes. An independent pre-drywall inspection and a final inspection before closing can catch construction issues while the builder can still fix them.

When is the St. George Parade of Homes?

The St. George Area Parade of Homes is held each February by the Southern Utah Home Builders Association. The 2026 Parade ran February 13–22 and featured 31 homes.

Where are new homes being built near St. George, Utah?

Major areas include Desert Color in south St. George, the Coral Canyon/Sienna Hills and Green Spring areas of Washington, and Hurricane, which has the most land of the nearby cities.

Should I bring my agent to the first model-home visit?

Yes. Many builders want your agent with you or registered at your first visit, so contact Kyle Wipf before you tour.
